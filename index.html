How the count is calculated

If attended / total is at least the minimum, classes you can miss = floor((attended - minimum / 100 x total) / (minimum / 100)).

If you are short, classes to attend in a row = ceil((minimum x total - 100 x attended) / (100 - minimum)).

By subject

Same rule for each subject. Attended classes cannot be more than the total.

About

Student-built for VIT. Private, on this device, with no ads and no account.

On this device

  • Your courses, CGPA, and attendance stay in local storage in this browser.
  • Nothing is uploaded. There is no account and no server.
  • There are no ads.
  • Reset on a tab clears that saved data. Your theme choice stays.

VITCalc is an unofficial student project. Check your own regulations for the rules that apply to you.
